Cell 1 — Family/Fidelity

“Am I being faithful?”

Commit to Christ, rule of life, share conviction with household, seek reconciliation.

Framework note

This cell begins with the individual spending time alone with God — an act of personal fidelity before anything else. Everything starts at this point: there is no good in doing all of the external works while not being right in your own soul.
