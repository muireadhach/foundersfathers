Cell 9 — Town/Stewardship

“Am I building well?”

Support local business, connect problems to solutions, build communication infrastructure.

Scripture anchors (36)
  • Acts 4:32-35 there was not a needy person among them — the connected community
  • Isaiah 58:6-12 repairer of the breach, restorer of streets to dwell in — restoration as vocation
  • Micah 4:4 every man under his own vine and fig tree — Washington's own recurring citation
  • Zechariah 8:3-5 the restored town — streets full of old men and women, boys and girls playing
  • Leviticus 25:23-28 the land is mine; you are strangers and sojourners with me — the root of Basil's stewardship claim
  • Leviticus 25:35-37 if your brother becomes poor, support him; take no interest from him
  • Deuteronomy 15:1-11 there will be no poor among you, if only you obey — the promise Acts 4:34 fulfills
  • Deuteronomy 24:14-15 pay the poor laborer his wages the same day
  • Deuteronomy 26:12-13 the tithe of the third year for the Levite, the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow
  • Ruth 2:8-16 Boaz's field — a local economy that makes deliberate room for the poor
  • Nehemiah 5:14-19 Nehemiah refuses the governor's food allowance and feeds a hundred and fifty at his table
  • Proverbs 13:22 a good man leaves an inheritance to his children's children — building for those who come after
  • Proverbs 14:31 whoever oppresses a poor man insults his Maker
  • Proverbs 22:22-23 do not rob the poor, or crush the afflicted at the gate
  • Proverbs 28:27 whoever gives to the poor will not want
  • Isaiah 32:8 he who is noble plans noble things, and on noble things he stands
  • Isaiah 61:4 they shall build up the ancient ruins; they shall repair the ruined cities
  • Isaiah 65:21-23 they shall build houses and inhabit them; plant vineyards and eat their fruit — the restored town
  • Jeremiah 22:13-17 woe to him who builds his house by unrighteousness, who makes his neighbor serve for nothing
  • Ezekiel 16:49 the guilt of Sodom: excess of food, prosperous ease, and she did not aid the poor and needy — naming causes
  • Amos 8:4-6 you who trample on the needy, making the ephah small and the shekel great
  • Micah 2:1-2 they covet fields and seize them, houses and take them
  • Malachi 3:5 against those who oppress the hired worker in his wages
  • Luke 3:10-14 John's civic ethics by trade — share your tunic, collect no more than authorized, extort no one
  • Luke 4:18-19 good news to the poor; liberty to the captives
  • Luke 16:19-31 the rich man and Lazarus — Chrysostom's On Wealth and Poverty text
  • Luke 19:1-10 Zacchaeus — half my goods to the poor, fourfold restitution; salvation come to this house
  • 2 Corinthians 8:9-15 he became poor so that you might become rich; that there may be equality
  • Galatians 2:10 remember the poor — the very thing I was eager to do
  • 1 Timothy 6:6-10 the love of money is a root of all kinds of evils
  • James 1:27 religion pure and undefiled: to visit orphans and widows in their affliction
  • James 5:1-6 the wages you kept back by fraud cry out — riches rotted, hoarded in the last days
  • Revelation 21:1-4 the holy city coming down — the telos of all building
  • Psalm 41:1 blessed is the one who considers the poor
  • Psalm 72:12-14 he delivers the needy when he calls, the poor and him who has no helper
  • Psalm 112:5-9 he has distributed freely, he has given to the poor
