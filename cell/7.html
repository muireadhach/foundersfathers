Cell 7 — Town/Fidelity

“Am I being faithful?”

Church engagement, city council presence, meet business owners, share vision.

Scripture anchors (31)
  • Jeremiah 29:4-7 seek the welfare of the city where I have sent you — fidelity in exile
  • Matthew 5:14-16 a city on a hill; salt and light — the church public without power
  • Proverbs 11:10-11 when the righteous prosper, the city rejoices — the town blessed through its faithful
  • Daniel 6:1-10 faithful public service under hostile scrutiny — presence without compromise
  • Genesis 18:22-33 Abraham pleads for the city — if ten righteous are found there
  • Exodus 18:21 able men who fear God, trustworthy, hating a bribe — the qualifications for civic office
  • Deuteronomy 16:18-20 judges in all your towns; justice, and only justice, you shall follow
  • 2 Samuel 23:3-4 he who rules over men justly, ruling in the fear of God
  • Proverbs 14:34 righteousness exalts a nation
  • Proverbs 24:11-12 rescue those being taken away to death — the duty to intervene
  • Proverbs 29:2 when the righteous increase, the people rejoice
  • Proverbs 31:8-9 open your mouth for the mute; defend the rights of the poor and needy — the Ambrose posture
  • Isaiah 1:17 seek justice, correct oppression, bring justice to the fatherless, plead the widow's cause
  • Jeremiah 22:15-16 he judged the cause of the poor and needy — is not this to know me?
  • Daniel 3:16-18 we will not serve your gods — faithful refusal within faithful service
  • Daniel 4:27 Daniel to the king: break off your sins by practicing righteousness — the prophet before power
  • Amos 5:10-15 they hate him who reproves in the gate; establish justice in the gate
  • Micah 6:8 do justice, love kindness, walk humbly with your God — the refrain's own source
  • Zechariah 7:9-10 render true judgments; do not oppress the widow, the fatherless, the sojourner, or the poor
  • Matthew 14:3-4 John to Herod: it is not lawful for you — truth spoken to power
  • Matthew 22:21 render to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's
  • Acts 5:29 we must obey God rather than men
  • Acts 16:37-38 Paul asserting his citizenship — civic standing used, not despised
  • Romans 13:1-7 governing authorities as God's servants for your good; pay taxes, respect, honor
  • 1 Timothy 2:1-2 prayers for kings and all in high positions, that we may lead a peaceful and quiet life
  • Titus 3:1-2 submissive to rulers, ready for every good work, showing courtesy to all people
  • 1 Peter 2:13-17 honor the emperor; live as people who are free, as servants of God
  • Hebrews 11:13-16 strangers and exiles on the earth, seeking a better country — the two-cities tension in one passage
  • Philippians 3:20 our citizenship is in heaven
  • John 17:14-18 not of the world, yet sent into the world
  • Psalm 122:6-9 pray for the peace of Jerusalem; for the sake of my brothers I will seek your good
